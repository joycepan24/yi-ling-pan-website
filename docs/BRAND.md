# Brand Foundation

## Role of this document

This is a durable editorial and visual brief, not a source of biographical facts. It defines how the site should feel and communicate while leaving all personal claims to approved content.

## Brand intent

Present Yi Ling Pan as a thoughtful, credible, generous educator working in bilingual/multilingual education. The site should make professional thinking and practical teaching resources easy to find, read, and share.

## Desired qualities

- Warm, calm, and intellectually serious
- Clear rather than promotional
- Reflective, practical, and respectful of learners
- Internationally legible without flattening linguistic or cultural specificity
- Professional enough for colleagues and institutions, welcoming enough for educators and families

## Voice

Use first person only where Yi Ling Pan has personally authored or approved the words. Prefer precise, plain language; short paragraphs; concrete examples; and respectful uncertainty where reflection is still evolving.

Avoid inflated claims, generic motivational language, jargon without explanation, and unsupported superlatives such as “leading,” “award-winning,” or “expert.”

## Bilingual and multilingual content

- Treat each language as a complete reading experience, not a decorative translation.
- Label a page's language clearly and set the correct HTML language attribute.
- Preserve names, quotations, and pedagogical terms accurately; add explanation when useful for readers outside the context.
- Do not assume translations are exact equivalents. Link translated counterparts only after they have been reviewed.
- Plan for different text lengths and reading directions in layouts.

## Audience priorities

1. Educators seeking ideas, reflections, or usable materials
2. Professional peers and collaborators assessing teaching and research interests
3. Institutions or event organizers seeking a clear professional overview
4. Learners and families, when content is intentionally written for them

## Content hierarchy

The home page should orient visitors to current professional focus and offer clear paths to writing, resources, and an approved professional profile. Individual article and resource pages should foreground title, context, date, language, and a useful next action.

## Visual direction

Favor generous whitespace, excellent typography, restrained color, and images with pedagogical meaning. Photography and illustrations must be licensed or owned, include useful alternative text, and never reveal students or classroom-sensitive material without appropriate permission.

## Current visual expression

The implemented site uses the `YI-LING PAN` wordmark, a warm neutral base, near-black text, muted lavender/butter/sage accents, and an editorial serif paired with a highly readable sans-serif. See [Visual Direction](VISUAL-DIRECTION.md) for the approved visual system; revise it only through a focused, approved visual change.
